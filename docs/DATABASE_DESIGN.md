# Database Design - SixOrbit ERP & Inventory

This document details the database schema, tables, fields, relationships, and constraints.

---

## 1. Schema Conventions
- All primary keys use `UUID` (autogenerated client-side or database-side `gen_random_uuid()`).
- Every table includes tracking fields:
  - `created_at`: DateTime (default now)
  - `updated_at`: DateTime (updated automatically)
  - `created_by`: UUID (foreign key to `User`, optional)
  - `updated_by`: UUID (foreign key to `User`, optional)
  - `deleted_at`: DateTime (for soft delete support, nullable)
- Appropriate indexes are created on foreign keys and frequently queried fields (e.g. `sku`, `email`, `doc_no`).

---

## 2. Table Directory

### A. Core Administration & Organization
- `companies`: Global tenant representation (name, logo, registration details, GSTIN, registered address).
- `branches`: Branches under the company (e.g., Vellore, Chennai).
- `outlets`: Sales outlets belonging to branches (e.g., Vellore Retail Outlet).
- `warehouses`: Storage locations. Linked to branches.
- `warehouse_locations`: Subsections within a warehouse (e.g., Cold Storage, Main Floor).
- `racks`: Storage racks within locations.
- `bins`: Bins within racks.

### B. User Authentication & Authorization (RBAC)
- `users`: User accounts (email, password hash, status).
- `roles`: Predefined and custom roles (Super Admin, Sales Manager, Accountant, etc.).
- `permissions`: Individual permission flags (e.g., `invoice:create`, `stock:adjust`, `finance:read`).
- `role_permissions`: Joins `roles` and `permissions`.
- `user_roles`: Joins `users` and `roles`.
- `sessions`: Active login sessions.

### C. Master Data
- `categories`: Product categories.
- `subcategories`: Product subcategories.
- `brands`: Product brands.
- `units`: Measurement units (e.g., SqFt, Box, Kg, Pcs).
- `unit_conversions`: Conversion rates (e.g., 1 Box = 10 Pcs).
- `products`: Product master details (name, SKU, base barcode, HSN/SAC, base price, min stock, status).
- `product_variants`: Variants of products (size, color, weight).
- `product_images`: Gallery paths for product items.
- `product_barcodes`: Multiple barcodes associated with products/variants.
- `price_lists`: Pricing sets.
- `price_list_items`: Prices for products in specific price lists.
- `tax_rates`: Tax definitions (CGST, SGST, IGST rates).

### D. Customer & Vendor Management
- `customers`: Customer accounts (name, email, phone, credit limit, customer group link).
- `customer_groups`: Customer classification (Retail, Wholesale, Distributor).
- `customer_addresses`: Multi-address mapping (billing, shipping, custom dispatch).
- `vendors`: Vendor accounts.
- `vendor_addresses`: Vendor locations.

### E. Inventory & Warehouse Management
- `stock`: Current quantity per product variant, warehouse, rack, and bin.
- `stock_batches`: Batch tracking details (batch number, manufacturing date, expiry date, rate).
- `stock_serial_numbers`: Individual item serial tracking.
- `inventory_transactions`: Log of all movements (source document type, doc ID, warehouse).
- `inventory_ledger`: Transaction ledger lines representing + or - quantity.
- `stock_adjustments`: Physical verification and quantity correction headers.
- `stock_adjustment_items`: Adjustment detail lines.
- `stock_transfers`: Inter-warehouse movement headers.
- `stock_transfer_items`: Inter-warehouse movement detail lines.
- `stock_requests`: Request for warehouse items from sales outlets.
- `stock_reservations`: Temporary reservations for pending sales orders.
- `stock_blocking`: Blocked inventory for QA or damage assessment.
- `minimum_stock_levels` / `reorder_rules`: Threshold definitions for auto-replenishment.

### F. Sales Workflow
- `quotations` & `quotation_items`: Sales quotes.
- `sales_orders` & `sales_order_items`: Confirmed sales order sheets.
- `invoices` & `invoice_items`: Sales invoices (contains the full transportation, billing, shipping, GST, and totals fields matching the SixOrbit reference).
- `delivery_notes` & `delivery_note_items`: Goods delivery challans.
- `sales_returns` & `sales_return_items`: Returned items log.
- `credit_notes` & `credit_note_items`: Financial return/credit adjustments.

### G. Purchase Workflow
- `purchase_requisitions` & `purchase_requisition_items`: Requisitions for stock.
- `purchase_orders` & `purchase_order_items`: Orders sent to vendors.
- `goods_receipts` & `goods_receipt_items`: Inbound stock documents (GRNs).
- `purchase_bills` & `purchase_bill_items`: Vendor invoices.
- `purchase_returns` & `purchase_return_items`: Purchase returns sent to vendors.

### H. Finance & Double-Entry Accounting
- `chart_of_accounts`: Account master registry (Assets, Liabilities, Revenue, Expenses).
- `ledgers`: Account balance sheet registers.
- `journal_entries`: Accounting entry headers (doc number, description, date).
- `journal_entry_items`: Debits and credits lines. Debits must equal credits in each journal entry.
- `payments`: Cash/bank receipts and payments.
- `payment_allocations`: Mapping payments to outstanding sales/purchase invoices.
- `receipts`, `expenses`, `income`: Standard office operational entries.
- `tax_transactions`: Detailed recording of output and input GST per invoice.

### I. Governance & Approvals
- `approval_workflows`: Workflow definitions per document type (Invoice, Expense, PO, Stock Adjustment).
- `approval_workflow_steps`: Defined approval stages and roles.
- `approval_requests`: Pending request items.
- `approval_history`: Historic logs of approvals/rejections.
- `notifications`: Alerts, approval tasks, and system messages.
- `audit_logs` & `activity_logs`: Entity-level logs recording old values and new values for changes.
- `attachments`: File uploads linked to invoices, products, or POs.
- `numbering_sequences` & `document_series`: Auto-sequencing counters.
- `settings`: Global configuration flags.
